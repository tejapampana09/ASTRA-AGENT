### Insights from: *Testery*

The Testery was a section at Bletchley Park , the British codebreaking station during World War II . It was set up in July 1942 as the "FISH Subsection" [ 1 ] under Major Ralph Tester , hence its alternative name. [ 2 ] Four founder members were Tester himself and three senior cryptanalysts: Captain Jerry Roberts , Captain Peter Ericsson and Major Denis Oswald . All four were fluent in German. From 1 July 1942 on, this team switched and was tasked with breaking the German High Command's most top-level code Tunny after Bill Tutte successfully broke Tunny system in Spring 1942.
